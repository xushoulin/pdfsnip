How to delete pages from a PDF

  1. Open your file. Drag the PDF onto the box above — it opens locally, nothing is uploaded.
  2. Mark pages for deletion. Click page numbers, or type ranges such as 1-2, 8-10.
  3. Delete and download. You get a new PDF with those pages removed. Your original file stays exactly as it was.

Tip: extract the pages you want to keep instead

If a document has 100 pages and you only need three, it is often faster to extract the pages you want than to delete the 97 you don't. Same privacy.html, same quality.
