Privacy Policy

The short version: your documents never leave your device.

Your files

Every tool on PDFSnip — extract, delete, rotate, merge and split — runs entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. When you open a PDF, it is read from your disk into your browser's memory, processed locally, and saved back to your disk.

We cannot see your files even if we wanted to. There is no upload step and no server that receives your documents. You can verify this: disconnect from the internet after loading the page and every tool still works.

What we collect

  • We do not require an account, email address or any personal information.
  • We do not store documents, page selections or results.
  • If anonymous usage analytics are enabled in the future, they will never include file contents or names, and this page will be updated.

Third-party services

The page loads the open-source pdf-lib library from a public CDN. Loading a library does not transmit your files or any document data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@pdfsnip.app.
