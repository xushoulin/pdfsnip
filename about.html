About PDFSnip

Free PDF page tools that respect your documents.

Why we built this

Every "free online PDF tool" we tried had the same catch: to extract five pages from a contract, you had to upload the whole document to somebody's server. For a tax form, a medical record, or a client agreement, that never felt right.

PDFSnip takes a different approach: every tool runs entirely in your browser. When you open a PDF here, it is read from your disk into your browser's memory, processed by JavaScript on your own device, and saved back to your disk. There is no upload step, because there is no server to upload to. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and everything still works.

What you get

  • Extract — copy selected pages into one new PDF (open the tool)
  • Delete — remove unwanted pages (open the tool)
  • Rotate — fix sideways scans (open the tool)
  • Merge — combine PDFs in any order (open the tool)
  • Split — separate a PDF into many files (open the tool)

All tools are free, require no account, and add no watermarks. Pages are copied byte-for-byte, so text stays selectable and images keep their original resolution.

How it works under the hood

PDFSnip is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, using the open-source pdf-lib library loaded from a public CDN. It is a static site — there is no backend, no database, and no analytics that could see your documents.
